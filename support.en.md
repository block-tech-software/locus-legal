## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I share my location with someone?
Add the person from the **Connections** tab using the **+** button. Your location is only shared with people you explicitly allow.

### How do I stop sharing my location?
Go to **Settings → Privacy → Location Sharing** and turn it off. You can turn it back on at any time.

### Why does Locus ask for location access "Always"?
So the people you allow can keep seeing your location while the app is in the background. If you prefer, choose **While Using the App** in **iOS Settings → Locus → Location**; background sharing will then stop.

### Who can see my location?
Only the connections you have allowed. Your location data is never shared with third parties for marketing purposes.

### How do I block someone?
Open the conversation with that contact and use the menu **(⋮)** in the top-right corner to block them. Blocked contacts can no longer interact with you.

### How do I report inappropriate content or a user?
Use the report option in the app or email us. Reports are reviewed within 24 hours, and accounts that violate our rules may be permanently banned.

### I'm not receiving notifications.
Make sure notifications are allowed in **iOS Settings → Notifications → Locus** and that Focus / Do Not Disturb is not blocking them.

### How do I change my password?
Go to **Settings → Change Password**.

### How do I delete my account and data?
Go to **Settings → Advanced → Delete Account** and confirm. All personal data is permanently deleted within 30 days. If you can no longer access the app, email us from the address linked to your account.
